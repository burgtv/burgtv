What to look for in an IPTV player

  • Your devices — according to its Google Play listing, TiviMate is designed for Android TV and not optimized for phones and tablets; BurgTV has apps for TV, Android and iPhone/iPad
  • Supported sources — M3U and Xtream Codes everywhere; BurgTV also plays a personal Jellyfin server and Portals (MAC + URL)
  • Big-screen features — TV guide, catch-up, timeshift, recording and Multi-View
  • Family — parental controls with PIN, minimum age and time limits
  • Transparency — a serious player provides no content

Free or Premium in BurgTV

On TV (Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV) the free version includes 1 playlist, live TV, a 3-day guide, 10 favorites and parental controls. Premium (license at app.burgtv.com, up to 5 devices across TV and Android on one account) adds unlimited playlists, Movies and Series, catch-up, a 14-day guide, timeshift, Multi-View, PiP, recording and profiles. On iPhone and iPad there is a 7-day trial, then Premium as an App Store purchase. On Android every feature is included until November 10, 2026; from that day the same limits as on the TV apply, and Premium is activated with the website license (5 devices across TV and Android). Anyone using the Android app before November 10, 2026 gets 3 months of Premium as a gift.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best IPTV player?

It depends on your devices and the features you need. TiviMate is designed for Android TV; IPTV Smarters is available on many platforms (on iPhone/iPad as “Smarters Player Lite”); BurgTV has apps for Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV, Android and iPhone/iPad. All of them play M3U and Xtream Codes.

Does BurgTV work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes, it is on the App Store: 7-day free trial, then Premium as an App Store purchase. The license bought on the website covers 5 devices across TV and Android, not iOS.

Can I switch from TiviMate to BurgTV?

Yes: add the same M3U playlist or Xtream Codes login from your source in BurgTV, on first launch from the Welcome screen or via Settings > My Lists > Add playlist.

Do these players include channels?

No. They are neutral players: you must connect your own legitimate source.

Is BurgTV free?

Yes, on TV and Android, with 1 playlist, live TV, a 3-day guide and parental controls; Premium unlocks the rest on 5 devices across TV and Android. On Android everything is included until November 10, 2026, with 3 months of Premium as a gift for earlier users.
