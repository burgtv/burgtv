How to open an M3U file (and what it is)

Got an M3U file or link and don’t know how to open it? You need a player (media player). Here’s what an M3U is and how to play it on Fire TV, Google TV, Android, iPhone and iPad with BurgTV. You bring your own source: BurgTV provides no content.

Download BurgTV All features

What is an M3U file

An M3U is a plain text file containing a list of streams or media (channels, films, music) as links. It holds no content itself: it’s just the “list”. You need a player that reads that list and plays the streams. The M3U8 variant is the same thing in UTF-8 (also used for HLS). Your legitimate provider gives you a .m3u file or an M3U URL.

What you need to open it

An IPTV/M3U player like BurgTV, in the spirit of VLC or Kodi. You connect your playlist and the player shows and plays what’s inside. BurgTV opens M3U links and .m3u files, Xtream Codes logins and also a personal Jellyfin server.

How to open an M3U, step by step

  1. Download BurgTV on your device: Fire TV, Google TV / Android TV, Android phone or tablet, iPhone/iPad.
  2. On the TV, add the playlist on first launch from the Welcome screen, or later from Settings > My Lists > Add playlist.
  3. Choose M3U and paste the full playlist link, or pick the .m3u file.
  4. Confirm: BurgTV reads the list and shows your channels ready to watch.

M3U as a link or a file?

If you have a URL (starts with http…), paste it in full: it’s the easiest way, because the list is read straight from your provider’s link. If you have a saved .m3u file, pick it as a file. If your provider gives you host, username and password, use Xtream Codes (see the Xtream Codes guide): the TV guide comes in automatically.

TV guide (EPG) for your M3U

If the playlist contains the guide address (url-tvg), BurgTV loads it by itself. Otherwise you can enter an external XMLTV URL in the TV Guide settings. On the TV the guide covers 3 days for free and up to 14 days with Premium. Full steps in how to add EPG to an IPTV playlist.

Not just M3U: Jellyfin and Portal

Running your own personal Jellyfin server? That’s the third way, next to M3U and Xtream Codes: you add it from Add playlist too, and BurgTV plays your media. Read Jellyfin on Fire TV with BurgTV. The list also shows Portal (MAC + URL): it’s just a technical format for sources of that kind.

Common problems

  • Won’t open / empty list: check the link is complete and valid (copy it in full).
  • Some channels won’t start: that depends on your source or connection, not the player.
  • .m3u file: add it in BurgTV with Add playlist, don’t open it from the file manager.
  • Can’t add a second playlist on the TV: the free version includes 1; Premium makes them unlimited.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is an M3U file?

A text file with a list of links to streams or media. It holds no content: it’s just the list a player uses to play them.

What’s the difference between M3U and M3U8?

M3U8 is an M3U encoded in UTF-8 and also used for HLS streaming. For the user it barely matters: BurgTV opens both.

How many M3U playlists can I add?

On the TV and on Android the free version includes 1 playlist; with Premium they’re unlimited. On Android everything is included until November 10, 2026. On iPhone and iPad there’s a 7-day free trial.

My M3U has no TV guide. What can I do?

If the playlist has no url-tvg, enter an external XMLTV URL in BurgTV’s TV Guide settings.

Is opening an M3U legal?

Yes. The player is neutral and legal; legality depends on your source. Only use legitimate playlists and subscriptions.

Can I open an M3U without installing an app?

You need a player that reads the playlist. BurgTV is a free download and opens M3U files and links on TV, Android, iPhone and iPad.
