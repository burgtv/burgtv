Guide · EPG XMLTV

How to add a TV guide (EPG XMLTV) to your playlist

The TV guide (EPG) shows what is on each channel now and over the next few days. In BurgTV it loads by itself with Xtream Codes or with an M3U playlist that points to it; otherwise you add an XMLTV URL yourself. BurgTV does not provide the guide: you bring the source, guide included.

What an EPG is and where it comes from

EPG stands for Electronic Program Guide. It is almost always a file in XMLTV format: a list of channels, each with an ID, plus programs with a start and end time. BurgTV is a neutral media player: it reads the guide that comes from your source, or from an XMLTV URL you have the right to use, and shows it next to the channels in your playlist. It does not sell, host or provide channels or guides.

Three ways to get the guide (TV app)

  • Xtream CodesConnect your playlist with host, username and password and the guide arrives automatically from your source’s server.
  • M3U with url-tvgIf your M3U playlist contains the url-tvg or x-tvg-url attribute, the guide loads by itself together with the channels.
  • External XMLTV URLIf the playlist does not point to any guide, enter an XMLTV URL yourself in the TV Guide settings.

Add an XMLTV URL, step by step

  1. Get the XMLTV guide URL from your source (providers often list it next to the playlist). It must be an address you have the right to use.
  2. On Fire TV, Google TV or Android TV, open BurgTV and go to the settings, TV Guide section.
  3. Enter the XMLTV URL carefully, with no spaces, and confirm.
  4. Wait for it to load: the first time can take a while, depending on the file size and your connection.
  5. Open the guide and check that programs appear next to the channels and that the times are right.

How channels and programs are matched

In an XMLTV file every channel has an ID; in an M3U playlist every channel has a name and often a tvg-id. BurgTV uses this data to link each channel to its programs. When the playlist ID and the guide ID are the same, matching is straightforward; when they differ, that channel may stay without listings. The most reliable fix is a guide made for your playlist, usually the one from the same source. Want to understand how a playlist is built? Read how to open an M3U file.

Times off? Use Time shift

If programs show up too early or too late, for example because the guide uses a different time zone, open the TV Guide settings and use Time shift. You can correct each playlist separately by a number of hours between -12 and +12. It is a manual correction: set it once and it applies to that playlist.

Time shift is in the TV app (Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV).

TV guide on Free and Premium (TV app)

Tonight on TV uses the guide to show you prime time on your channels. With Premium you can set a reminder (Set Reminder) and schedule recordings from the guide, for personal use and respecting your source’s rights: see how to record from the guide.

On phones and tablets

On Android phones and tablets and on iPhone and iPad, the guide is shown per channel inside Live TV: pick a channel and see what is on. The full guide grid is in the TV app.

Common problems

  • Empty guide: check the XMLTV URL (one wrong character is enough), make sure your source actually provides a guide, and keep in mind that a very large file may fail to load. With Xtream Codes the guide depends on your source’s server.
  • Wrong times: set the playlist’s Time shift in the TV Guide settings.
  • Some channels without listings: the playlist IDs and the guide IDs do not match, or the guide does not contain that channel.
  • Guide too short: the free version shows 3 days; Premium up to 14, if your guide contains enough days.

Frequently asked questions

Does BurgTV provide the TV guide?

No. BurgTV is a neutral player: the guide comes from your source (Xtream Codes or url-tvg in the playlist) or from an XMLTV URL you have the right to use.

Why is my EPG empty?

Usually the XMLTV URL is wrong, the file is too large or the source does not provide a guide. Check the address again in the TV Guide settings.

The guide times are wrong. What can I do?

Use Time shift in the TV Guide settings: correct the playlist by a number of hours between -12 and +12.

Why do some channels have no guide?

The channel ID in the playlist (tvg-id or name) does not match the one in the guide, or the guide does not include that channel.

How many days of guide can I see?

In the TV app, 3 days for free and up to 14 days with Premium, if your guide contains them.

Is there a guide on the phone app too?

Yes: on Android, iPhone and iPad you see the channel’s guide inside Live TV. The full grid is in the TV app.
