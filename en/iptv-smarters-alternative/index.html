BurgTV or IPTV Smarters: an honest comparison

Updated 11 October 2026 · Checked with BurgTV 1.3.7 (TV) · 1.2.1 (Android) · 1.0.15 (iPhone/iPad)

IPTV Smarters is one of the best-known IPTV players. If you are looking for an alternative, here is how BurgTV compares, with facts we checked on 11 October 2026 on each app’s official pages and store listings, including where Smarters is the better choice. Both are players: neither provides channels or content.

In short

Choose IPTV Smarters if you need an app directly on a Samsung or LG television, on Apple TV or on a Windows or Mac computer: BurgTV has no app there. Choose BurgTV if you watch on Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV, Android or iPhone and want timeshift, Multi-View, recordings, profiles with a Kids profile, Portal and Jellyfin sources, with no ads.

Where IPTV Smarters is stronger

  • More platforms: native apps for Samsung and LG televisions, Apple TV, Windows and Mac, and a web player.
  • Local files: according to its App Store listing it also plays media stored on the device; BurgTV opens playlists only from a link.
  • Years on the market: it is widely known and you will find many guides about it online.

Where BurgTV is stronger

  • Timeshift up to 120 minutes on TV, Android and iPhone: pause and rewind live TV (Premium on TV and Android). See IPTV timeshift.
  • On the TV: Multi-View with up to 4 channels, recordings, profiles and a Kids profile (Premium).
  • More source types: Stalker/MAG Portal and Jellyfin, also on iPhone.
  • No ads, usage statistics only with your consent, and one developer in Switzerland: who we are.
  • Watch out for look-alike apps: several apps with similar names exist on the stores. IPTV Smarters’ official developer is WHMCS Smarters; the official BurgTV apps are the ones linked from this website, on download.burgtv.com and the App Store.

Moving from IPTV Smarters to BurgTV

  1. Take the same details you use in Smarters: the M3U link, or the Xtream Codes server, username and password, or the portal address with the MAC.
  2. Install BurgTV on your device: see the install guide.
  3. Add the source in Settings > My Lists > Add playlist on the TV, or in Settings > Content sources on phones. The TV guide arrives by itself with Xtream Codes.
  4. If you keep both apps open at once, remember that each one uses a connection of your access.

Frequently asked questions

Is BurgTV a good alternative to IPTV Smarters?

If you watch mainly on Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV, Android or iPhone, yes. On Samsung or LG televisions, Apple TV or computers, Smarters has apps and BurgTV does not.

Can I use the same login in BurgTV?

Yes. The same M3U link, Xtream Codes login or portal you use in Smarters also works in BurgTV.

Is IPTV Smarters on Google Play?

Its official website says it is not, and offers the Android app as an APK. Apps with similar names on the stores may come from other developers.

Does BurgTV work on Apple TV?

There is no BurgTV app for Apple TV. From an iPhone or iPad you can send the picture to an Apple TV with AirPlay.

Do BurgTV or IPTV Smarters provide channels?

No. Both are players: you add a source you have the right to use.
