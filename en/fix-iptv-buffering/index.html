How to fix IPTV buffering

Buffering (the picture freezing and reloading) ruins the experience. Here’s why it happens and the real fixes on Fire TV, Google TV, Android, iPhone and iPad with BurgTV, a neutral player: you bring your own source. Honestly, it’s almost always the source or connection, not the player.

Why buffering happens

Buffering happens when data arrives slower than the video plays it. Causes, in order of frequency: your source/provider (overloaded server, especially during live events), your connection (weak Wi‑Fi, not enough bandwidth), the player settings (video decoding, buffer size) and the device. The player is almost always the last cause.

Quick fixes (in order)

  1. Restart your router and device: this solves most connection issues.
  2. Use Ethernet or 5 GHz Wi‑Fi near the router instead of 2.4 GHz.
  3. Try another channel or stream: if only some freeze, it’s the source, not you.
  4. On the TV, go to Settings > Playback > Video decoding and choose Automatic, Hardware or Software (free). If the picture is green or distorted, try Software.
  5. With Premium, also on the TV, raise the Buffer size (Settings > Playback) to 32 or 64 MB so the player keeps more data in reserve.

  • Video decodingAutomatic, Hardware or Software, free on the TV. Green or distorted picture: try Software.

When it’s NOT the player

  • Only during games or live events: the source server is overloaded. Try another legitimate source.
  • Every app buffers: it’s your internet connection, not the app.
  • Fine on the phone but not on the TV: the TV’s Wi‑Fi is weaker, so use a cable or a repeater.
  • Only freezes in Multi-View: each tile uses one connection of your source, and many subscriptions only allow 1 or 2.

Check your connection

Run a speed test on the device. As a rough guide, an HD stream needs 10‑15 Mbps steady and 4K about 25 Mbps. If bandwidth fluctuates, buffering is unavoidable with any player. An Ethernet cable fixes most cases on TV boxes.

What about a VPN?

A VPN can slow things down or be throttled. Turn it off to check, or pick a closer server. Legality always depends on your legitimate source.

Frequently asked questions

Why does IPTV stutter only in the evening?

In the evening and during live events source servers are more loaded. It’s a source issue, not the player: try another legitimate source or a different time.

Is buffering the player’s fault?

Almost never. In order: source/provider, connection, settings, device. Switching players rarely helps; fix connection and source first.

Which video decoding should I choose?

Leave it on Automatic. If the picture is green or distorted, on the TV try Software in Settings > Playback > Video decoding. This setting is free.

Can I increase the buffer?

Yes, on the TV with Premium: Settings > Playback > Buffer size, from 8 to 64 MB (default 16). 32 or 64 MB help with unstable sources but can’t make up for a slow connection.

Wi‑Fi or cable for IPTV?

Ethernet is far more stable, especially on TV boxes and for 4K. On Wi‑Fi, stay on 5 GHz near the router.

How much bandwidth does IPTV need?

Roughly 10‑15 Mbps steady for HD and about 25 Mbps for 4K. Stability matters more than peak speed.
