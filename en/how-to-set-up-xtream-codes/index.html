Guide · Xtream Codes

How to set up Xtream Codes (step by step)

Got an Xtream Codes host, username and password from your provider? Here’s how to enter them in BurgTV on Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, with the TV guide (EPG) loaded automatically. You bring your own source.

Download BurgTV All features

What is Xtream Codes

Xtream Codes is a way to connect your source with a host, username and password instead of a plain M3U URL. The upside: the credentials usually stay the same (some providers change their M3U link from time to time), and channels, movies, series and the TV guide (EPG) arrive already organized. It’s only a connection method — you provide the legitimate source.

What you need

  • Host / server URL (e.g. http://example:port)
  • Username and password from your legitimate subscription
  • BurgTV installed on your device (TV, phone or tablet)

Step-by-step setup (TV)

  1. Download BurgTV on Fire TV, Google TV or Android TV and open it.
  2. On first launch pick your source from the Welcome screen, or go to Settings > My Lists > Add playlist.
  3. Choose Xtream Codes and enter the host, username and password exactly as your provider gave them.
  4. Confirm: channels and the TV guide load automatically. On TV, Movies and Series are part of Premium.

On phones and tablets

The app for Android phones and tablets and the one for iPhone and iPad also accept Xtream Codes logins: add a new playlist and choose Xtream Codes. You add the source on each device — there is no automatic transfer between TV and phone.

TV guide (EPG) with Xtream

With Xtream Codes the TV guide comes automatically from your provider’s server. If it’s missing or incomplete, add an external XMLTV URL in the TV Guide settings and fix the times with Time shift (from -12 h to +12 h per playlist). On TV the guide covers 3 days for free, up to 14 days with Premium. More in how to add an EPG guide.

Xtream Codes, M3U or Jellyfin?

  • Xtream Codes: if your provider gives you a host, username and password. TV guide included.
  • M3U: if you only have a URL or a file. See how to open an M3U file.
  • Jellyfin: if you run your own personal Jellyfin server with your own videos. See Jellyfin on Fire TV.
  • There is also the technical Portal type (MAC address + URL) for sources that use that format.

Common problems

  • Login error (“Authentication failed”): recheck host, username and password with no spaces and make sure the subscription is active.
  • Works on one device but not another: many subscriptions allow only 1 or 2 connections at a time.
  • Empty TV guide: add your provider’s XMLTV URL in the TV Guide settings.
  • Buffering: usually down to the connection or the source. See how to reduce buffering.

Frequently asked questions

What is Xtream Codes?

A way to connect your source with a host, username and password instead of an M3U URL. Channels, on-demand content and the TV guide arrive already organized.

Xtream Codes or M3U — which is better?

Xtream Codes if you have a host, username and password: TV guide included and credentials that usually stay the same. M3U if you only have a link. BurgTV supports both, plus personal Jellyfin servers.

“Authentication failed”: what do I do?

Recheck the credentials (no spaces), confirm the subscription is active, and make sure it isn’t already in use on more devices than your plan’s connections allow.

Can I add several Xtream accounts?

Yes. On TV the free version handles 1 playlist; with Premium playlists are unlimited.

Is setting up Xtream Codes legal?

Yes. The player is neutral; legality depends on your source. Only use legitimate subscriptions.
