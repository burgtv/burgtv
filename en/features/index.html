Every BurgTV feature, device by device

BurgTV is a neutral IPTV player for Fire TV, Google TV, Android TV, Android phones, iPhone and iPad. Here is everything it does, where it works and what needs Premium. You bring your own source: BurgTV provides no content.

Features

  1. 01TV, phone and tablet

    The same experience on TV, phone and tablet

    In the living room on Android TV, Fire TV and Google TV, in your hand on iPhone, iPad and Android. The same polished interface on every screen; some features, such as Multi-View and recordings, are built for the TV.

    • TV
    • Phone
    • Tablet

  2. 02TV

    TV guide, Tonight on TV and catch-up

    The guide shows what's on now and next, channel by channel, without leaving the video. Tonight on TV gathers prime time and, if your source offers catch-up, you can rewatch shows that already aired.

    • EPG guide
    • Tonight on TV
    • Catch-up

  3. 03TV · Premium

    Timeshift up to 60 minutes and recordings

    Pause live TV, go back up to 60 minutes and resume whenever you like. You can also record a show for personal use, or schedule a recording from the guide.

    • Pause live TV
    • Recordings
    • Reminders

  4. 04TV · Premium

    4-screen Multi-View and Picture-in-Picture

    Follow up to four live streams on one screen (two on devices with little memory) and choose which one to hear; each window uses one connection of your source. With Picture-in-Picture the video keeps playing in a small window while you do something else.

    • Multi-View
    • PiP

  5. 05TV · Premium

    Sports with reminders

    Today's events by sport and competition, with times in your time zone. A reminder alerts you before the start, so you never miss kick-off.

    • Event calendar
    • Reminders

  6. 06TV, phone and tablet

    Trailers and Check availability

    Movie and series pages with plot, cast and trailer. With Check availability BurgTV searches your playlists for the title.

    • Trailers
    • Check availability

  7. 07TV · Premium

    Kids profile and parental controls

    Everyone gets their own profile. The Kids profile shows only titles suited to the chosen age thanks to age ratings; unrated titles stay hidden. Parental controls protect with a PIN and add a daily limit and a time window.

    • Age ratings
    • Parental PIN

  8. 08TV, phone and tablet

    Your sources, in your language

    Connect Xtream Codes, M3U playlists, your Jellyfin server or a Portal. The app speaks 21 languages. You bring the source: BurgTV only plays what you add.

    • 21 languages

    Save episodes from your source to your phone, when your source allows it, and watch them even without a connection: on the train, on a plane, on holiday.

On the TV: watching live

In the app for Fire TV, Google TV and Android TV, live TV is free. Two playback engines (ExoPlayer with FFmpeg, and libVLC), up to 4K when the stream and the device support it, multiple audio and subtitle tracks. If a channel drops, BurgTV retries on its own.

  • Channel surfingSwitch channels with the remote. Fast full-screen channel switching is Premium.
  • TimeshiftPause and rewind live TV with a 15, 30 or 60-minute buffer. Not on HLS channels. Premium.
  • Catch-upReplay past programmes if your source offers an archive. Premium.
  • Picture-in-PictureKeep a channel playing in a small window while you browse. Premium.

Learn more: timeshift on the TV.

TV Guide and Sports

The TV Guide loads automatically with Xtream Codes or from the playlist’s url-tvg; you can also enter an external XMLTV URL in the TV Guide settings. Free shows 3 days, Premium up to 14. If times are off, the per-playlist Time shift setting goes from -12 to +12 h.

  • Grid by channel and time slot, with programme details
  • Tonight on TV for prime time at a glance, plus the Discover section
  • Set Reminder on an upcoming programme (Premium)
  • Sports: today’s events; BurgTV looks for matching channels in your playlist. Reminders with Premium

Movies and series

On the TV, the Movies and Series sections need Premium and present the titles from your own source in a tidy layout.

  • Detail pages with plot, cast and trailer
  • Check availability: BurgTV searches your playlists for the title
  • Recently added and Continue Watching to pick up where you left off

Multi-View and recordings

With Multi-View you watch up to 4 channels at once (2 on devices with less than 1.5 GB of RAM). Each tile uses one connection from your source, and many subscriptions allow only 1 or 2. Recordings start from the player or are scheduled from the guide (Scheduled recordings) and are saved to the TV device’s internal storage. To share at home there is a small local server with a QR code, reachable only on your home network, with an MP4 copy on request. Both features are Premium and TV-only.

Recordings are for personal use, respecting the rights of your source.

Profiles, Kids profile and parental controls

  • Parental controls (free): 4-digit PIN, Minimum Age (All, 7+, 12+, 14+, 16+, 18+), block a single channel by holding OK in the list
  • Daily time limit and time window per profile
  • Separate profiles for everyone in the house (Premium)
  • Kids profile (“Kids”, Premium): maximum age 12 or lower; unrated titles stay hidden; leaving it requires the PIN

Age ratings come from public TMDB data: only title and year are sent, no personal data. Full guide: parental controls in BurgTV.

Supported sources and 21 languages

BurgTV plays M3U playlists (URL or file), Xtream Codes accounts, your personal Jellyfin server and Portal (MAC + URL). On the TV you add them at first launch or from Settings > My Lists > Add playlist. The app comes in 21 languages, including English, Italian, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Turkish, Dutch, Polish, Russian and Arabic. No ads; analytics are optional and stay off until you consent.

Frequently asked questions

What is free in BurgTV?

On the TV: 1 playlist, live TV, a 3-day guide, 10 favourites, parental controls and video decoding choice. On Android, for now, every feature is included without a purchase: the Android app is still in a testing phase and is not on Google Play yet.

What does Premium unlock on the TV?

Unlimited playlists, Movies and Series, catch-up, a 14-day guide, timeshift, Multi-View, Picture-in-Picture, recordings, profiles and Kids profile, reminders, buffer size and fast full-screen channel switching.

Does the website licence work on iPhone?

No. The app.burgtv.com licence is for TVs (up to 5). On iPhone and iPad, Premium is bought in the App Store after a 7-day trial and works on both with the same Apple ID.

Does BurgTV work without a source?

No. BurgTV is only a player and contains no channels or movies: you need your own source, such as an M3U playlist, an Xtream Codes account, a Jellyfin server or a Portal.

Which devices does it run on?

Fire TV Stick and Cube, Google TV Streamer, boxes and TVs with Android TV or Google TV, Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad. Newer Fire TV devices running Vega OS (for example the Fire TV Stick 4K Select, the 2nd-generation Fire TV Stick HD and the new 3rd-generation Fire TV Stick 4K) cannot install Android apps or APK files. BurgTV works on Fire TV devices running Fire OS, the Android-based system: for example the Fire TV Stick 4K Max, the Fire TV Cube and earlier Fire TV Stick and Fire TV Stick 4K models.
